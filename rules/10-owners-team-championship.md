# Owners / Team Championship

10.1. Team Points are earned by the top 3 finishing drivers for each race. For example, Team A Drivers finish 1st, 4th, 9th, 10th, and 15th, The driver's finishing 1st, 4th, and 9th would earn the team points for that race. The team with the most points at the end of the 15-race season wins the team championship.

10.1.1. Penalties received by drivers will affect the team points as well.

10.2. It is the Team Owner's / Captains responsibility to review the league rules with the team and make sure the team has a good understanding of the SCSRA Rule Book. Once reviewed, the team owner / captain must communicate with a member of the admin team that their team understands and agrees with the SCSRA Rule Book. There is a dedicated channel for Rule Book questions that Team Owners / Captains will have access to.

10.3. Team Owner – All teams will designate a team owner to work with the admin team. There is a designated channel reserved for team owners to reach out to admins relating to questions about post-race penalties or any other questions the team may have.
