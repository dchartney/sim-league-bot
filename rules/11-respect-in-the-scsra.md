# Respect in the SCSRA

11.1. On and off the track (iRacing chat, SCSRA Discord, SCSRA YouTube, SCSRA Facebook, and all other active Social Media Platforms, etc.), the SCSRA is committed in all areas to provide a fun and safe environment that is free from harassment. It is against league policy and the iRacing Member Code of Conduct for any member to verbally abuse, verbally harass or physically harass any member of the SCSRA on any social media platform. It is also against league policy and iRacing Member Code of Conduct to discriminate against another member on the basis of individuals race, color, religion, national origin, genetic information, disability, sex, age, sexual orientation, gender (including gender identity and expression), marital status, protected veterans' status, citizenship status or any other characteristic protected by applicable law. Immediate removal from the league is at the sole discretion of the Admin Team.
