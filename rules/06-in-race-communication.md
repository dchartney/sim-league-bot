# In race communication

6.1. Keep radio communications limited to race chatter so drivers can call out to Race Control and Admins as needed.

6.2. The Stock Car Sim Racing Association (SCSRA) is committed in all areas to provide a fun environment that Is free from harassment. It is against league policy and the iRacing Member Code of Conduct for any member to verbally abuse, verbally harass or physically harass any member of the SCSRA on any social media platform. It is also against league policy and iRacing Member Code of Conduct to discriminate against another member based on individuals' race, color, religion, national origin, genetic information, disability, sex, age, sexual orientation, gender (including gender identity and expression), marital status, protected veterans' status, citizenship status or any other characteristic protected by applicable law. Members engaging in harassment will be penalize as follows:

6.2.1. Automatic DQ from the current race session

6.2.2. 1 race suspension

6.2.3. 15-point penalty

6.2.4. Driver will also be placed on probation and called into the SCSRA Trailer

6.2.5. Any additional infractions of 6.2 will result in immediate and permanent removal from the SCSRA League and Discord.

6.3. We will allow drivers to go back and forth for a bit to try and solve the issue. If Admins / Race Control issue a verbal warning and the back and forth does not stop, the drivers that were warned mic's will be muted for the remainder of the race and subject to a possible 15-point penalty at the admin's discretion. Admin / Race Control warnings will be when they step in after the argument has lasted too long or at their discretion.

6.4. Under iRacing's Code of Conduct, these races can be protested by drivers and / or by viewers.
