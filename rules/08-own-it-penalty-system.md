# 'Own It' Penalty System

8.1. 'Own It' Penalty System – If a driver causes a caution, the driver must own up to it and call out their causing of the incident when Race Control asks. Race Control / Admin will then give you an EOL with 2 laps to go under caution. If you get 2 EOL's in 1 Race, you will be asked to park it for the remainder of the race.

8.2. If multiple drivers are involved and no one 'Owns It', it will be reviewed after the race. There will then be an EOL handed down to the driver at fault or the wreck will be deemed a 'Racing Incident'.

8.3. In the Post Race review by admins, if you are found guilty of a non-claimed cause of caution, the driver will get an EOL for the start of the following race, not counting towards the next race total. If the reviewed EOL happens to be the driver's cumulative total of 2 of that race, they will be regulated to the end of current lap and assessed at 10 point penalty. Driver will recieve and EOL for the next race, not counting towards cumilative total for that race.

Example: Driver causes a caution and claims. They recieve EOL. Driver causes a second caution. but does not claim, rule 8.3 will be enforced.

8.3.1 If unclaimed caution comes before Stage Break, driver will forfit all stage points earned.

8.4. Driver Causes more than 1 caution and does not own either/any:

8.4.1. After post-race review by league admins, if found at fault for 2 or more unclamaimed cautions, the driver will be disqualified from that race and issued a EOL for the following race. This EOL will count towards the drivers 2 for that race.

8.4.1.1. Example: Driver a Causes 2 incidents in Race 1 and does not 'Own It' for either of them and did not receive any EOL's. Following post-race review, they will receive 2 EOL's for that race. The driver would then be disqualified, receive 0 points for the race. The driver will start the next race with 1 EOL counting towards the 2 for that race.

8.5. Last Lap EOL Penalty

8.5.1. If you cause a 2+ car incident (including yourself) on the last lap, you will finish one position behind the car you contacted.

8.5.2. If you cause a multi car incident, you will finish one spot behind the lowest finisher of all the cars involved in the incident unless said car is 1 lap or multiple laps down.

8.6. Penalties / Penalty Review – Penalties for the race prior may be handed down up to the green flag of the start of the respective series' next race. Drivers may submit rule infractions to the admin team. Penalties / Rule infractions will not be handed out for the week prior once the next week's race goes green.

8.7. If a driver gets disqualified from 5 races in 1 season, that driver will be removed from the league.
