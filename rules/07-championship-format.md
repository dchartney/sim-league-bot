# Championship Format

7.1. Season will consist of 15 points races. 10 Regular Season Races and 5 Playoff races.

7.2. The top 12 drivers (wins and points) at the end of Race 10 will be locked into the playoffs. The format is as follows.

7.2.1. Round 1 – Race 11 and 12 – Bottom 4 Playoff Drivers will be eliminated.

7.2.2. Round 2 – Race 13 and 14 – Bottom 4 Playoff Drivers will be eliminated.

7.2.3. Round 3 – Race 15 – Championship 4 – Top Finisher of the 4 drivers is champion.

7.3. Playoff eligibility – Drivers must compete in a min of 8 races during the regular season to be playoff eligible.

7.4. Winning a regular season race will lock you into the playoffs unless there are more than 11 winners. If we have more than 11 winners, we will use the tie breaker rules in Section 5.

7.5. Regular Season Champion is locked into the playoffs and would be the 12th playoff driver if they fail to win a race. They will also receive 5 Playoff Points.

7.6. At the start of Round 1 (Races 11 and 12), the 12 playoff drivers will have their points reset to 1000 PLUS playoff points.

7.7. At the start of Round 2 (Races 13 and 14), the 8 remaining playoff drivers will have their points reset to 2000 plus playoff points.

7.8. At the start of Round 3 (Race 15), the 4 remaining playoff drivers will have their points reset to 3000 points with no bonus points. Highest finishing driver of the Championship 4 will be declared champion, unless post-race infractions occur.

7.9. Playoff Drivers are required to have the SCSRA Playoff Banner on for races they are playoff eligible. Banner must be removed once the driver is eliminated from the playoffs. The same rule is in effect for 1.5.5.
