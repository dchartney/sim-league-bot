# Penalties

9.1. Any driver that wins a race and receives a post-race penalty resulting in a loss of points will have the win revoked and given to the next eligible driver. Said driver that had the win revoked will not be locked into the playoffs and will finish as the last car on their respective finishing lap unless rules specify otherwise.

9.2. Intentional wrecking – If you intentionally wreck someone:

9.2.1. Green / Yellow Flag – You will be disqualified from the race, penalized 25 driver points and receive a 1 race suspension. If found guilty of this during post-race review, you will be disqualified from the previous race, penalized 25 driver points, and receive a 1 race suspension. You will also be placed on probation for the remainder of your SCSRA Career. Any additional infractions of rule 9.2 will result in immediate removal from the SCSRA League and Discord.

9.2.2. After Checkered Flag / Cool Down Lap – If Race Control or admins witness or see in post-race review a driver intentionally wreck someone after the checkered flag / on the cool down lap after crossing the start / finish line, you will be disqualified from the race, receive 0 points for this race, receive a 25-point driver points penalty, and receive a 1 race suspension. You will also be placed on probation for the remainder of your SCSRA Career. Any additional infractions of rule 9.2 will result in immediate removal from the SCSRA League and Discord.

This is unsportsmanlike and looks disgraceful on the broadcast. Keep it off the track and message / discuss with the other driver in one of the SCSRA Post Race Channels or via private message. We ask that ALL SCSRA drivers drive their cars orderly to pit road at the race's conclusion and not stop on track to exit the vehicle. This looks good on broadcast rather than a bunch of wrecking.

9.3. The "Russ Marlin" Rule – If you stop on the track to bring out a yellow, you will be disqualified from the race, penalized 25 driver points and receive a one race suspension. If found guilty of this after the race, you will be disqualified from the previous race, receive 0 points for that race, receive a 25-point driver point penalty, and receive a 1 race suspension. You will also be placed on probation for the remainder of your SCSRA career. Any additional violation of rule 9.3 will result in immediate removal from the SCSRA League and Discord.

9.4. Superspeedway Racing – There will be absolutely no passing below the yellow line. If found guilty during green flag racing, you will get a pass-through penalty. If found guilty during post-race review, you will be placed as the last car on your respective lap and receive a 5-point penalty for each instance.

9.5. Car / Truck Number – Drivers must run the number on their car / truck that was signed up with. If you are not running the proper number when the race goes green, the following penalties apply:

9.5.1. 1st offense – Warning from the admin team. Will be documented.

9.5.2. 2nd offense – EOL for that race

9.5.3. 3rd offense – Parked for any subsequent race until the issue is corrected.

9.6. If caught crowding another driver on initial start or any subsequent restart either post-race or during the race, penalties will be at the admin discretion up to removal from the league. This is not to discourage hard racing, but to promote clean restarts for the best competitive racing we can.

We don't want to hand down these penalties, we ask that you drive the correct number that you signed up with. It ensures there are no issues on the broadcast and makes things easier for them. If you want to change your number, please reach out to a member of the admin team for assistance.
