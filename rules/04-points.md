# Points

4.1. The winner will receive 40 points, declining by 1 point per position. To receive points, you must complete a minimum of 5 laps for oval track and 3 laps for road courses.

4.2. Regular Season Bonuses:

4.2.1. Race Win – Locked into the playoffs (Except under rule 3.4.3).

4.2.2. Race Winner – 3 points.

4.2.3. Lap Led – 1 Point

4.2.4. Led Most Laps – 1 Point

4.2.5. Pole Position – 1 Point

4.2.6. Finish race with 0 incident points – 3 Points (25% of laps must be completed)

4.3. Playoff Bonuses

4.3.1. Race Winner – 3 playoff points.

4.3.2. Stage Break Winner – 1 Playoff Point

4.3.3. Regular Season Champion – 5 Playoff Points

4.4. The bonus points mentioned in Section 4.2 and 4.3 will carry over through round 1 and 2 in the playoffs.
