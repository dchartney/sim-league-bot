# Driver Requirements

1.1. Must use a steering wheel.

1.2. Must have a working mic to communicate with Drivers, Race Control, and admins during the race. Must be able to hear directions during the race as well.

1.3. Must be in SCSRA Discord to get race info, schedule, points, etc.

1.3.1. All SCSRA Drivers MUST be in the SCSRA Discord for Races. Drivers may be in their respective team room (Max. 5 Drivers per room). Drivers may be pulled during the race for in race interviews as well as post race interviews.

1.4. Must run the correct number from signups on car / truck (See section 9: Penalties)

1.5. Must run the series banner posted in Discord under Rules and Announcements, #banners.

1.5.1. ARCA Series Banner

1.5.2. NextGen Banner

1.5.3. MUST HAVE CORRECT BANNER ON BY THE 3rd RACE OF THE SEASON OR YOU WILL NOT ACCUMULATE POINTS. There is a 10-point penalty for every race with the incorrect banner.

1.6. Must have a Trading paints account with an uploaded paint scheme. It is free: https://www.tradingpaints.com/

1.6.1. There is also a link in Discord under Rules and Announcements, #links

1.7. Must fill out SDK Gaming Driver Profile form for Broadcasters. Link in 1.7.1.

1.7.1. https://www.sdk-gaming.co.uk/free-app-driver-information/

1.7.2. There is also a link in Discord under Rules and Announcements, #links

1.8. League Fees must be paid up before the 2nd race of the season in each series. No pay, No play. You will be removed from the league in iRacing until league fees are paid.

1.8.1. A link to pay is in discord under rules and announcements, #payments

1.9. No Substitute drivers allowed. Only authorized drivers are allowed to race. Any driver caught having a substitute driver race for them will be removed from the league.
