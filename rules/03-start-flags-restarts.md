# Start / Flags / Restarts

3.1. All races start on 'green flag'. Leader must maintain pace speed until the flagman throws the green flag.

3.2. All restarts are at the leader's discretion within the restart zone.

3.2.1. The leader must maintain pace speed up to the marked restart zone. Once the leader enters the zone, he can go at any time. If the leader chooses not to go on his own choosing in the Restart Zone, the flagman will throw the green flag when determined by iRacing.

3.2.2. At tracks without a marked restart zone, the leader may go at any time once the pace car has all 4 tires below the white line.

3.2.2.1. Front row cars on restart may not aggressively crowd the other driver.

3.2.2.2. All restarts are reviewed post-race, this is at the admin's discretion. See section 9.6

3.3. Drivers may not go below the white / yellow line on starts / restarts before crossing the start / finish line.

3.4. Drivers cannot change lanes until after you have crossed the start / finish line, unless you are avoiding a wreck.

3.4.1. The guilty will receive a drive through penalty when race control is available. If race control is not available, please refer to 3.4.2

3.4.1.1. Drivers may self-police this and may elect to take the drive thru penalty under green flag conditions if they feel they violated rule 3.4.

3.4.1.1.1. If you are on pit serving the drive thru penalty and a caution comes out, this will count as clearing the penalty.

3.4.2. If found guilty in post-race review, the following penalty shall apply:

3.4.2.1. 1 offense – 5-point penalty

3.4.2.2. 2 offenses – 10-point penalty (on top of 5-point initial penalty, 15 points total)

3.4.2.3. 3+ offenses – 15-point penalty (on top of 15-point initial penalty, 30 points total), dropped to last place points and an EOL for start of next race (counts towards 2 EOL's to park for that race).

3.4.3. Winner with Post Race Infractions: if you are the winner of the race and receive a post-race infraction, Lane Change Violations (Rule 3.4), the win will be revoked and given to the next eligible driver. You will also receive the following points penalties:

3.4.3.1. 1 Offense – Last place position on Lead lap, 5-point penalty

3.4.3.2. 2 Offenses – Last place position on Lead Lap, 10-point Penalty (on top of 5-point initial penalty, 15 points total)

3.4.3.3. 3+ Offenses – Last place position at end of the race, 15-point penalty (on top of 15-point previous penalty, 30 points total), EOL for start of the Next Race (Counts towards 2 EOL's to park for that race).

3.5. iRacing will oversee all yellow flags.

3.5.1. A caution may be thrown by admin / Race Control on a Restart if there is an incident involving 2 or more drivers and iRacing did not throw a yellow.

3.5.2. If a yellow comes out within 5 laps of a stage break, it will be considered the Stage Caution.

3.6. Stage Cautions will be thrown by the admin / Race Control approximately 2 seconds after the leader crosses the line at the start of the next scheduled lap. For example, Stage is at the end of Lap 30, Caution will come out at the start of lap 31.

3.6.1. To receive stage points, Drivers must:

3.6.1.1. Be inside the Top 10 at the time of the stage caution (Field will be frozen).

3.6.1.2. Cross the line and be on the racing surface at the conclusion of the stage and cannot be on or entering pit road.

3.6.1.3. Make it to the open pit lane under their own power to receive any stage points. If the driver fails to do so, they lose their stage points, and the next eligible driver receives the points.

3.6.2. If a driver claims EOL for caution within the final 5 laps of the stage, thus causing the stage to end early (3.5.2), that driver becomes ineligible to receive stage points

3.6.2.1. If caution that caused stage to end early is unclaimed and reviewed post-race and a driver is determined to be at fault, any stage points received by said driver will be vacated. Stage points will then be handed down to the next eligible driver(s).

3.6.3. Pits will be closed once the leader hits 2 laps to go in stage. For example, if the stage ends of lap 50, the pits will be closed once the leader completes Lap 48 and begins lap 49.

3.6.4. Race control may adjust the timing of the caution dependent on the track to minimize the number of laps that the field remains under caution.

3.7. Black Flags: The only Black Flags that will be cleared are:

3.7.1. Passing under yellow due to an incident where a damaged vehicle is involved and is not maintaining pace speed due to said damage (This does not mean you can pass vehicles under yellow for any other reason, you must maintain your position).

3.7.2. Black Flags will also be cleared for unjustified penalties (See 3.7.3)

3.7.3. If you wreck or avoid a wreck near pit road entrance / through pit road and receive a black flag for 'Unsafe Pit Entrance' and / or 'Speeding', you may get back on the racing surface after the merge cone. Standard pit road exit still applies, black flags will be cleared after you return to the racing surface. iRacing might give you an EOL on top of this which will not completely clear. It will continue telling you to "Let Car #X by", which is usually the last car in line. Advise Race Control if this happens.

3.7.4. Meatball black flags will only be cleared within 5 laps of the end of the race.

3.7.5. All cleared black flags will be reviewed after the race. If found to have a black cleared for any other reason than 3.7.1 or 3.7.2 and found guilty, you will receive a 15 points penalty. You will also start the next race with an EOL.

3.8. Pacing: All drivers must maintain pace speed and be within a reasonable distance to the vehicle in front of you under yellow. Please catch the field as quickly and safely as possible. If you cannot catch the field before pit stops begin or before the restarts, please advise Race Control. To avoid black flags, please request an EOL.

3.9. Restarts:

3.9.1. When pacing with 1 lap until green, you cannot lay back to try and get a jump on another driver. You must maintain no more than a car length to the vehicle in front of you. This is approximately .1 - .2 on your relative black box. Failure to do so will result in the following penalties:

3.9.1.1. 1 offense – Verbal warning from admin team. Will be documented.

3.9.1.2. 2+ offenses – 5-point penalty for each instance

3.9.2. The leader has lane choice on race start and all restarts. Lane choice must be declared over iRacing chat coming off of pit road on race start and coming to '1 lap to go' on all restarts.

3.9.3. On restarts, the leader is the control car coming to the restart zone. Drivers cannot change lanes until after you have crossed the start finish line, unless you are avoiding a wreck. Please refer to Rule 3.4 for penalties.

3.9.4. Once the pace car pulls onto pit road coming to the restart, the leader must maintain + / - 2MPH of pace speed coming to the 'Restart Zone'. For example, if the pace speed is 50 mph, the leader must maintain no lower than 48 MPH and no higher than 52 mph.

3.9.5. Penalties if caught after the race during post race review:

3.9.5.1. 1 Offense – 5 Point Penalty

3.9.5.2. 2 Offenses – 10 Point penalty (On top of 5 Point initial penalty, 15 points total), Moved to last position on lap that you finished.

3.9.5.3. 3+ Offenses – Last place points on the Grid, EOL For the start of the next race (Counts towards 2 EOL's to park for that race)

3.9.6. Winners with Post Race Infractions: If you are the winner of the race and receive a post race infraction (ex. Restart Violation(s) (Rule 3.9)), the win will be revoked and given to the next eligible driver. You will also receive the following points penalties:

3.9.6.1. 1 offense – 5 Point Penalty and Last place position on the lead lap.

3.9.6.2. 2 Offenses – 10 point Penalty (On top of 5 Point initial penalty, 15 points total) and last place position on lead lap.

3.9.6.3. 3+ offenses – 15 point penalty (On top of 15 points initial, 30 points total), Last place position on Grid and EOL for the start of the next race (Counts towards 2 EOL's to park it for the race). This is your warning.

3.10. Pitting:

3.10.1. During cautions, all drivers must enter pit road single file and to the right (left on some road courses). You can only enter the middle / left lane when you are approximately 5 pit stalls away from your assigned pit stall. Drivers may not drive through more than 2 boxes entering their pit stall. Drivers may not drive through more than 2 boxes when exiting your pit stall. If you enter / exit and use more than 2 stalls, the penalties are as follows:

3.10.1.1. 1 offense – Verbal warning from admin team. Will be documented.

3.10.1.2. 2 + offenses – 5 point penalty (Each instance)

3.10.2. Under Green flag conditions, drivers pitting at the same time may enter pit road side by side. Drivers must get to single file as soon as possible on the right side (left on some road courses) of pit lane. You can only enter the middle / left lane when you are approximately 5 pit stalls away from your assigned pit stall. Drivers may not drive through more than 2 boxes entering their pit stall. Drivers may not drive through more than 2 boxes when exiting your pit stall. If you enter / exit your pit stall and use more than 2 boxes, the penalties are as follows.

3.10.2.1. 1 offense – Verbal warning from admin team. Will be documented.

3.10.2.2. 2 + offenses – 5 point penalty (Each instance)

3.10.3. Note: Black flags for "Exiting Closed Pits" may not be cleared by admin / race control.
